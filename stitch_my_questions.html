Original Query

“Does a missed check-out count as absent for payroll?”

check
HR's Official Answer

No. A missing check-out is flagged as an attendance exception, not an absence. Payroll still uses your scheduled hours for that day.

Your manager must correct the record within 3 working days, otherwise it is auto-closed at your scheduled shift end.
